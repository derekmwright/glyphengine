These are copies of the assets in `06-skinned` and `08-grass` rather than
references to them. Examples embed their assets with `go:embed`, which cannot
reach outside its own directory, and each example is meant to be readable and
copyable on its own — a reader lifting this one should not have to discover
that its character lives three directories away.

`character.glb` references `Textures/colormap.png` by relative URI, so the
directory layout is load-bearing.

The font ships as a generated atlas with no `.ttf`: Go Regular arrives through
`golang.org/x/image`, so `task font` regenerates it from a source the module
system fetches. See `examples/10-text/assets/ASSETS.md` for why that typeface
and not one of the OFL-licensed alternatives.

CC0 and BSD 3-Clause both impose no attribution requirement, which is why these
are usable in an MIT-licensed repository without adding an obligation
downstream consumers would inherit. Crediting Kenney and Quaternius is
nonetheless the decent thing to do, and this file is that credit.

Assets whose provenance cannot be established do not go in this repository,
regardless of how likely they are to be fine.
