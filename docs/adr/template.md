## Context

What problem or constraint requires a decision? What must remain true?
For a retrospective record, identify the existing sources and any unknown
history; the recorded date is not the original decision date.

## Decision

State the choice, its scope, and the contract future changes must respect.

## Alternatives

Describe the meaningful alternatives and why they do not fit these constraints.
Do not imply an alternative was historically evaluated without evidence.

## Consequences

Include benefits, costs, limitations, and any follow-up work or conditions that
would justify revisiting the decision.

## References and evidence

Link to related ADRs, code, capability docs, issues, and relevant checks or
measurements. Distinguish existing evidence from checks run for this change and
from validation still needed; do not invent results.
